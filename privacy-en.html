Privacy Policy

SoraStudio ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of users of PlaceAlarm ("the App") and handles personal information and location information appropriately.

1. Use of Location Information

The App uses location information to detect when you arrive at or leave a saved place and to alert you with an alarm when you arrive.

Location information is used to provide the location-based features of the App.

2. Data Storage

Data such as saved places in the App is managed on your device to provide the App's features.

We do not send or store your location information or saved place information on our own external servers.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide your location information or information about places saved in the App to third parties.

4. Location Permission

To use the App's arrival and departure detection features, you must allow the App to access your iPhone's location.

To detect arrivals at saved places while the App is running in the background, please allow the appropriate location access in your iPhone's settings.

5. In-App Purchases

The App uses Apple's StoreKit framework for purchases of Premium features.

Purchase processing and payment information are handled through Apple's services. We do not collect payment information such as credit card numbers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as necessary.

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will become effective when the updated policy is posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about the App, please contact us at the email address below.
