PlaceAlarm

Get an alarm when you arrive at a saved place

About PlaceAlarm

PlaceAlarm uses location information to detect when you arrive at a saved place and notifies you with an alarm.

PlaceAlarm helps you avoid missing your stop at a station or remember things you want to take care of when you arrive at places such as your home.

Key Features

  • Automatically detects arrival at saved places
  • Detects departure from saved places
  • Alerts you with an alarm when you arrive
  • Searches saved places by name, address, or memo
  • Adds, edits, and deletes saved places
  • Registers a place using your current location
  • Shows the location and arrival radius of saved places
  • Adjusts location detection accuracy

About Premium

Premium removes the limitations of the free version, allowing you to register and use more places.

  • Removes the limit on the number of saved places
  • Removes the limit on the number of alarm uses

Privacy

PlaceAlarm uses location information to detect your arrival at saved places and provide its alarm features.

Location information and other data used within the app are managed on your device to provide the app's features.

To use location-based features such as arrival and departure detection, you must allow PlaceAlarm to access your iPhone's location.
